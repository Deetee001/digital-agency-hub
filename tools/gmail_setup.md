# Gmail Setup — Email Outreach Tool

Claude can send emails directly through your Gmail account using the Gmail MCP integration already available in this session.

---

## How to Authenticate Gmail

In your Claude Code session, run:
```
/authenticate gmail
```
Or say: "Authenticate my Gmail account"

Claude will walk you through a one-time OAuth login. Once connected, you can say:

> "Send an email to [address] with subject [X] and body [Y]"

And Claude will send it for you.

---

## What Claude Can Do With Gmail
- Draft and send cold outreach emails
- Send follow-ups on a schedule
- Check replies and summarize responses
- Attach proposal PDFs

---

## Best Practices for Cold Email Deliverability

1. **Use a custom domain email** — `yourname@yourdomain.com` looks more professional than Gmail
   - Get a domain from Namecheap (~$10/yr) and set up Google Workspace ($6/mo) or Zoho Mail (free)
   
2. **Warm up a new email address** — send 5–10 normal emails first before blasting cold outreach

3. **Avoid spam trigger words** in subject lines:
   - Bad: "FREE website!!! MAKE MONEY!!!"
   - Good: "Quick question about [Business Name]'s online presence"

4. **Send in small batches** — 10–20 emails/day max when starting out

5. **Always include an unsubscribe line:**
   > "Reply with 'no thanks' if you'd prefer not to hear from me."

---

## Email Tracking (Optional)
- Use **Mailtrack** (free Gmail extension) to see when emails are opened
- Or **HubSpot Free CRM** which includes email tracking

---

## Bulk Email (When Volume Increases)
- **Mailchimp** — free up to 500 contacts
- **SendGrid** — free up to 100 emails/day
- These are better for newsletters; cold outreach should always be personal 1-to-1
