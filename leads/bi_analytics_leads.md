## Outreach Priority Order

1. **Nonprofits first** — grant season is always a good hook; they NEED impact data
2. **Healthcare practices** — HIPAA-aware data work, but high value
3. **Multi-location businesses** — they feel the pain of scattered data most acutely
4. **Manufacturing** — high ROI on efficiency analytics

## Notes on Nonprofit Outreach
- Many nonprofits have grants specifically for "capacity building" — your services qualify
- Best contacts: Executive Director, Program Director, or Development Director
- Lead with grant reporting / funder accountability angle
- Lorain County Foundation and Cleveland Foundation both fund capacity building grants — clients may have budget for this
