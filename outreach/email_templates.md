```
I'm a web designer based in the Oberlin area and I build clean, professional websites for local businesses — usually for a one-time cost between $299 and $599, with no ongoing fees unless you want maintenance.

A website helps you:
✓ Show up on Google when people search "[your type of business] near me"
✓ Get found instead of losing customers to competitors who do have websites
✓ Look professional and build trust before customers even walk in

I'd love to build something for you. Can I send over a quick mockup of what your site could look like — no obligation?

[Your Name]
[Your Phone]
[Your Email]
```

## Sending Instructions
1. Personalize [Business Name], [Owner Name], and any bracketed fields before sending
2. Log every email sent in `leads/crm_tracker.md`
3. Wait 7 days before following up
4. Max 3 follow-ups per lead before marking [X] Not interested
