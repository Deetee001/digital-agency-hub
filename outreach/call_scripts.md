# Phone Call Scripts

---

## Script 1 — Cold Call (No Website)

**Goal:** Get them interested in a free mockup or 10-minute follow-up call

```
YOU: "Hi, is this [Business Name]?"

THEM: "Yes."

YOU: "Hi, my name is [Your Name] and I'm a local web designer here in the Oberlin area.
     I was actually trying to find your business online and noticed you don't have a website yet.
     I help local businesses like yours get online so new customers can find you on Google.
     Is the owner available, or am I speaking with them?"

[If not owner → ask for owner's name and best time to call back]

[If owner] → 

YOU: "Great! I'll be quick — I know you're busy.
     I build websites for local businesses starting at just $299 — and I actually already
     put together a rough idea of what yours could look like.
     Would you be open to me sending it over so you can take a look? No pressure, totally free."

THEM: [Yes] → "Perfect! What's the best email to send it to?"
              → Log email, send Template 1 within the hour

THEM: [Maybe/Not sure] → "Totally understand. Can I call back at a better time?
                          When's usually a slower moment for you?"

THEM: [Not interested] → "No problem at all. If you ever change your mind, feel free to reach out.
                          Have a great day!"
```

## Script 2 — Cold Call (Outdated Website)

```
YOU: "Hi, I'm trying to reach the owner of [Business Name] — is that you?"

THEM: [Yes]

YOU: "Great. My name is [Your Name], I'm a local web designer in Oberlin.
     I actually visited your website recently and I noticed it's a bit outdated —
     I think it might actually be turning customers away rather than bringing them in.
     I specialize in modernizing local business websites. Would you be open to a quick
     10-minute call where I can show you what a refreshed version could look like?"

THEM: [Yes] → Schedule call. Prep a quick mockup/comparison beforehand.

THEM: [Not now] → "Totally fine. What's a better time? I promise I'll keep it under 10 minutes."
```

## Script 3 — Voicemail

```
"Hi, this is [Your Name], a web designer in the Oberlin area.
I was trying to find [Business Name] online and noticed there's no website yet —
I wanted to reach out because I help local businesses like yours get found on Google.

I'm going to send you a quick email with a free mockup of what your website could look like.
No commitment, just want to show you what's possible.

My number is [Your Phone] if you'd like to chat.
Have a great day!"
```

---

## Script 4 — Follow-Up Call (After Email)

```
YOU: "Hi, is this [Owner Name]? This is [Your Name] — I sent you an email about
     building a website for [Business Name] last week. Did you get a chance to see it?"

THEM: [Yes, saw it] → "Great! What did you think? Any questions I can answer?"

THEM: [No / Haven't seen it] → "No worries — I'll resend it right now.
                                It's just a quick mockup of what your site could look like.
                                Can I follow up in a couple days once you've had a chance to look?"

THEM: [Not interested] → "Totally understand. Is there anything specific that's a concern —
                          cost, timing? I want to make sure I'm not missing something."
```
