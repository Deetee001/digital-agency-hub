# SMS / Text Message Templates

Short, conversational, and action-oriented. Keep texts under 160 characters when possible.

---

## Text 1 — Initial Cold Text (No Website)

```
Hi, I'm [Name], a local web designer in Oberlin. I noticed [Business Name] 
doesn't have a website yet. I build simple sites starting at $299 that help 
customers find you on Google. Want me to send a free mockup? No commitment.
```

---

## Text 2 — Follow-Up (No Reply After 3 Days)

```
Hey, just following up — did you get my last message about a website for 
[Business Name]? Happy to do a free sample so you can see what it'd look like 
before deciding anything.
```

---

## Text 3 — After Voicemail

```
Hi [Owner Name], I just left a voicemail about building a website for 
[Business Name]. Here's a free mockup I put together for you: [LINK]. 
No pressure — just wanted you to see what's possible. -[Your Name]
```

---

## Text 4 — Facebook-Only Business

```
Hey! Noticed [Business Name] has a great Facebook page but no website. 
FB can disappear overnight — a site gives you something permanent + 
helps Google find you. I build them starting at $299. Want to see an example?
```

---

## Text 5 — Scheduling a Call

```
Hey [Name], this is [Your Name] the web designer. Would you have 10 minutes 
this week to chat about a website for [Business Name]? 
Morning or afternoon — whatever works for you!
```

---

## Text 6 — Proposal Follow-Up

```
Hi [Name], just sent over the proposal for [Business Name]'s website. 
Let me know if you have any questions — happy to jump on a quick call 
to walk through it. -[Your Name]
```

---

## Sending Instructions
- Use Twilio for automated/bulk texts (see `tools/twilio_setup.md`)
- Always personalize [Business Name] and [Your Name] before sending
- Never send more than 2 texts to the same number without a response
- Log all texts in `leads/crm_tracker.md`
- Best times to text: Tue–Thu, 10am–12pm or 2pm–4pm (avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons)
