# Discovery Questions — For Interested Prospects

Ask these 5 questions before building a mockup or proposal.

---

1. **What does your business do?** (In their words — use this language in the website copy)

2. **Who are your ideal customers?** (Locals? Students? Families? Tradespeople?)

3. **What do you want people to do when they visit your site?** 
   - Call you?
   - Come in?
   - Place an online order?
   - Book an appointment?

4. **Do you have any photos of your business, products, or team?** 
   (If not, we can use stock photos or schedule a quick photo session)

5. **Is there a competitor's website you like the look of?** 
   (Helps you understand their taste and expectations)

---

**Bonus questions if they're engaged:**
- Do you have a logo? (Or do you need one?)
- Do you have a domain name already?
- Who should we credit as the contact on the website?
- Are there any hours, services, or menu items we need to include?
