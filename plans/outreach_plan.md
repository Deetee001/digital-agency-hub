# Outreach Strategy & Action Plan

## Weekly Outreach Cadence

### Week 1 — Auto Shops (easiest to convert, high need for Google visibility)
**Targets:** S&S Auto, Johnson's Automotive, Gary's Body Shop, D&D Automotive, PC Automotive

**Day 1 (Mon):** Cold call all 5 auto shops. Leave voicemail if no answer.
**Day 2 (Tue):** Send cold email to any that didn't answer (Template 1).
**Day 3 (Wed):** Text any that haven't responded (Text Template 1).
**Day 5 (Fri):** Follow-up call to any still unresponsive.

---

### Week 2 — Restaurants & Diners
**Targets:** Main Street Grille, Catrinas, Ying's Asian Cuisine, Lorenzo's Pizzeria, Mutt & Jeff's

**Note:** Best times to call restaurants = 10am–11:30am (before lunch rush) or 2pm–4pm (after lunch, before dinner prep)

**Day 1:** Cold call all 5. Ask for owner by name if possible.
**Day 2:** Email follow-up to non-responders (Template 1).
**Day 3:** Text follow-up (Text Template 1).
**Day 5:** Second call attempt.

---

### Week 3 — Retail & Service Businesses
**Targets:** Knight Jack Cleaners, Main Street Antiques, Watson Hardware, Uptown Clothing, All Things Great

**Day 1:** Cold call or walk in (these are downtown — in-person is powerful here).
**Day 2:** Email follow-up.
**Day 3:** Text follow-up.

---

### Week 4 — Lorain & Elyria Expansion
**Targets:** Diso's Bistro, Scorcher's, Speak of the Devil, Wolfey's Bistro, Black River Tavern

---

## Contact Sequence Per Lead

```
Day 1:  First call (or walk-in if downtown)
Day 2:  Email (if no answer/response)
Day 4:  Text (if still no response)
Day 7:  Second call / follow-up email
Day 14: Final follow-up — "Checking in one last time"
Day 15+: Mark as [X] if no response, revisit in 60 days
```

## In-Person Strategy (Powerful for Downtown Oberlin)

For businesses on Main St / College St / W Lorain St:
1. Print a one-page "Free Website Mockup" flyer
2. Walk into the business and ask for the owner
3. Show a sample site on your phone/laptop
4. Leave a business card with your contact info
5. Follow up with email/text the next day

**What to say in person:**
> "Hi, I'm [Name] — I'm a local web designer and I noticed you don't have a website yet. 
> I actually put together a rough idea of what yours could look like. Could I show you real quick?"

## Proposal Process

Once a business is interested:
1. Ask 5 questions (see `outreach/discovery_questions.md`)
2. Build a quick mockup in Figma or Canva (1–2 hours)
3. Send the proposal email (Template 5)
4. Follow up by phone 2 days later
5. Close with a 50% deposit

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I don't have the budget" | "I have a starter option at $299 — less than a month of Facebook ads." |
| "I already have Facebook" | "Facebook can change or suspend your page at any time. A website is yours forever." |
| "I don't need it" | "How are people finding you right now? A site puts you on Google when they search." |
| "I'll think about it" | "Totally fair. Can I send you a free mockup first so you have something concrete to think about?" |
| "I'm too busy" | "I handle everything — you just answer 5 quick questions and I do the rest." |
