# Business Naming & Branding Ideas

## What the Business Does (For Reference When Naming)
- Web design & development for local businesses
- Business intelligence & data analytics
- Cloud database infrastructure setup
- Process automation
- Data migration, security, and reporting
- Analytics & impact measurement for nonprofits/organizations

The name needs to work for **both** a local small business owner getting their first website **AND** a nonprofit director looking for data infrastructure. It should feel professional, modern, and local without being too niche.

## Top Recommendation

- Both work for web clients AND enterprise/nonprofit analytics clients

- Professional enough for B2B pitches, approachable for small local shops
- NorthCoast Digital has strong regional identity for Ohio-based growth

## Domain Strategy

### Where to Buy
- **Namecheap.com** — cheapest, ~$8–$12/yr for .com
- **Google Domains (now Squarespace Domains)** — clean interface, ~$12/yr
- Avoid GoDaddy for the upsells

### Which TLD to Get
- `.com` is always first choice
- If `.com` is taken, try `.co`, `.io`, or `.digital`
- Examples: `northcoastdigital.com`, `northcoast.digital`, `northcoastdigital.co`

### Also Register
Once you pick a name, lock down:
- The `.com` domain
- The exact name on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Gmail

## Logo/Visual Identity Ideas
- Color palette: Deep blue + white + a bold accent (orange or teal) — professional and energetic
- Font style: Clean sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, or Poppins)
- Icon ideas: data node/graph that morphs into a web cursor; North Star; compass pointing forward
- Free logo tools: Canva, Looka.com (AI logo generator, free preview)
