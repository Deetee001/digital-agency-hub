# Business Plan — Local Web Design Agency

## Mission
Build affordable, professional websites for local businesses in Oberlin, Lorain, and Elyria, OH that have no web presence or outdated sites. Help them get found on Google and grow their customer base.

---

## Target Market
- **Primary:** Small local businesses in Oberlin, OH with no website (restaurants, auto shops, retail, service businesses)
- **Secondary:** Businesses with outdated websites in Lorain and Elyria, OH
- **Future:** Expand to surrounding counties (Medina, Huron, Cuyahoga)

---

## Competitive Advantages
1. **Local** — you're in Oberlin, you know these businesses, you can meet in person
2. **Affordable** — national agencies charge $3,000+; you charge $299–$1,500
3. **Fast** — most projects done in 1–2 weeks
4. **Personal** — ongoing relationship, they know who to call

---

## Tools & Stack

### Website Building
- **HTML/CSS/JS** — for simple, fast, cheap sites (no monthly platform fees)
- **WordPress** — for clients who want to edit their own content
- **Squarespace/Wix** — for clients who want drag-and-drop control
- **Hosting:** Netlify (free), Vercel (free), or Bluehost ($3/mo for clients)
- **Domains:** Namecheap (~$10/yr per domain)

### Outreach & CRM
- **Email:** Gmail + MCP integration for AI-assisted sending
- **SMS:** Twilio (see `tools/twilio_setup.md`)
- **CRM:** `leads/crm_tracker.md` (upgrade to HubSpot Free when volume increases)

### Design
- **Figma** (free) — mockups and wireframes
- **Canva** — quick graphics and logos
- **Unsplash / Pexels** — free stock photos
